## Definition of Divisibility

An integer $a$ is divisible by an integer $b$ (where $b \ne 0$) if there exists an integer $c$ such that:

$$
\Large
a = b \cdot c
$$

Notation:
- $b \mid a$ denotes "$b$ divides $a$" or "$a$ is divisible by $b$".
- $b \nmid a$ denotes "$b$ does not divide $a$".

Where:
- $\large a$ is the **Dividend**
- $\large b$ is the **Divisor** ($b \ne 0$)
- $\large c$ is the **Quotient** ($c \in \mathbb{Z}$)

$$
\textit{eg.}\\
\large
\begin{align*}
3 \mid 12 &\implies 12 = 3 \cdot 4 \quad (4 \in \mathbb{Z})\\
5 \nmid 14 &\implies \text{No integer } c \text{ satisfies } 14 = 5c
\end{align*}
$$

## The Division Algorithm

For any integer $a$ and any positive integer $b$, there exist unique integers $q$ (quotient) and $r$ (remainder) such that:

$$
\Huge
a = b \cdot q + r
$$

Where:
- $\large 0 \le r < b$
- $\large a$ is the **Dividend**
- $\large b$ is the **Divisor** ($b > 0$)
- $\large q$ is the **Quotient**
- $\large r$ is the **Remainder**

$$
\textit{eg.}\\
\large
\begin{align*}
a = 17, \; b = 5 &\implies 17 = 5(3) + 2 \quad (q = 3, \; r = 2)\\
a = -17, \; b = 5 &\implies -17 = 5(-4) + 3 \quad (q = -4, \; r = 3)
\end{align*}
$$

## Properties of Divisibility

Let $a, b, c, d \in \mathbb{Z}$:

### Reflexivity
Every integer divides itself:
$$
\Large
a \mid a \quad (\forall a \ne 0)
$$

### Transitivity
If $a$ divides $b$ and $b$ divides $c$, then $a$ divides $c$:
$$
\Large
a \mid b \land b \mid c \implies a \mid c
$$

### Linear Combination Property
If $a \mid b$ and $a \mid c$, then for any integers $x, y \in \mathbb{Z}$:
$$
\Large
a \mid (bx + cy)
$$

$$
\textit{eg.}\\
\large
\begin{align*}
3 \mid 6 \text{ and } 3 \mid 9 &\implies 3 \mid (6(2) + 9(3))\\
&\implies 3 \mid (12 + 27)\\
&\implies 3 \mid 39
\end{align*}
$$

### Multiplication Property
If $a \mid b$, then for any integer $c$:
$$
\Large
a \mid bc
$$

### Unit Divisors
The only divisors of $1$ are $\pm 1$:
$$
\Large
a \mid 1 \implies a = \pm 1
$$

### Mutual Divisibility
If $a \mid b$ and $b \mid a$, then:
$$
\Large
a = \pm b
$$
