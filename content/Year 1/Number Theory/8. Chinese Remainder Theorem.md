## Solving Linear Congruences

It has the form:

$$
\Huge
ax \equiv b \pmod{m}\\
\Large
\text{where:}\\
x < m \ and\ x \in \N
$$

* When $\gcd(a, m) \nmid b$, it has **no solutions**.
* When $\gcd(a, m) = 1$, it has **one/unique solution**.
* Let $d = \gcd(a, m)$. When $d \mid b$, it has **$d$ mutually incongruent solutions**

### Worked Example

Solve the Equation
$$
2x \equiv 3 \pmod 8\\
$$

Determine $d$ or the GCD of $a$ and the moduli $m$
$$
\begin{align*}
d&=\gcd(a, m)\\
d&=\gcd(2, 8)\\
d&=2
\end{align*}\\
$$

Check for divisibility between $d$ and $b$
$$
d \mid b\\
2 \nmid 3\\
$$
$$
\therefore\ \fbox{The linear equation has no solutions}
$$

## Chinese Remainder Theorem

Used to solve a set of different linear congruent equations with one variable but diffirent moduli, which are relatively prime (co-prime)

$$
\Huge
x \equiv a_1 \pmod{m_1}\\
x \equiv a_2 \pmod{m_2}\\
x \equiv a_3 \pmod{m_3}\\
...\\
x \equiv a_n \pmod{m_n}\\
$$

### Steps to solve CRT

1. Find $a$ and $m$

2. Determine if the GCD is co-prime
$$
\Large
\gcd(m_1, m_2) = \gcd(m_2, m_3) = ... = \gcd(m_{n-1}, m_{n})
$$

3. Solve for $M$
$$
\huge
M = \prod_{i=1}^{n}{(m_i)}\\
$$

4. Solve for $M_i$
$$
\huge
M_i = \frac{M}{m_i}\\
$$

5. Solve for $x_i$
$$
\huge
M_i x_i \equiv 1 \pmod{m_i}\\
\large
\text{For this one, solve $x_i$}\\
$$

6. Solve for $X$
$$
\Large
X = \sum_{i=1}^{n}(a_i m_i x_i) \mod M\\
$$

7. Find the general solution
$$
\huge
X + Mt\\
\large
\text{Where $t$ is a parameter, and $t \in \N$}
$$
