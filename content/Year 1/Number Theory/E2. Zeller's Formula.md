---
title: E2. Zeller's Formula
tags: major, number theory, mathematics, extras
---

$$
\Large
w \equiv q + \lfloor \frac{13}{5} (m+1) \rfloor + K + \lfloor \frac{K}{4} \rfloor + \lfloor \frac{J}{4} \rfloor + 5J \pmod{7}
$$

Where:
- $\large w$ is the day of the week
- $\large q$ is the day of the month
- $\large m$ is the month
- $\large K$ is the last 2 digits of the year
- $\large J$ is the first 2 digits of the year

### Worked Example

$$
\text{What day of the week is December 25, 2026?}
$$
$$
q = 25,
K = 26,
J = 20,
m = 12
$$
$$
\large
\begin{align*}
w &\equiv 25 + \lfloor \frac{13}{5} (12+1) \rfloor + 26 + \lfloor \frac{26}{4} \rfloor + \lfloor \frac{20}{4} \rfloor + 5(20) \pmod{7}\\
w &\equiv \lfloor \frac{13}{5} (13) \rfloor + \lfloor \frac{13}{2} \rfloor + \lfloor 5 \rfloor + 151 \pmod{7}\\
w &\equiv 195 \equiv 6 \pmod{7}\\
\end{align*}\\
\fbox{$w \equiv 6 \pmod{7}\\$} \text{ or } \fbox{Friday}
$$
