## Zeller's Congruence

$$
\Large
w \equiv q + \left\lfloor \frac{13}{5} (m+1) \right\rfloor + K + \left\lfloor \frac{K}{4} \right\rfloor + \left\lfloor \frac{J}{4} \right\rfloor + 5J \pmod{7}
$$

Where:
- $\large w$ is the day of the week ($0 = \text{Saturday}, 1 = \text{Sunday}, \dots, 6 = \text{Friday}$)
- $\large q$ is the day of the month
- $\large m$ is the month (March $= 3$, April $= 4, \dots,$ January $= 13$, February $= 14$ of previous year)
- $\large K$ is the year of the century ($\text{year} \bmod 100$)
- $\large J$ is the zero-based century ($\lfloor \text{year} / 100 \rfloor$)

---

## Worked Example

$$
\text{What day of the week is December 25, 2026?}
$$

Given:
$$
q = 25, \quad K = 26, \quad J = 20, \quad m = 12
$$

$$
\large
\begin{align*}
w &\equiv 25 + \left\lfloor \frac{13}{5} (12+1) \right\rfloor + 26 + \left\lfloor \frac{26}{4} \right\rfloor + \left\lfloor \frac{20}{4} \right\rfloor + 5(20) \pmod{7}\\[6pt]
w &\equiv 25 + \lfloor 33.8 \rfloor + 26 + 6 + 5 + 100 \pmod{7}\\[6pt]
w &\equiv 25 + 33 + 26 + 6 + 5 + 100 \pmod{7}\\[6pt]
w &\equiv 195 \equiv 6 \pmod{7}
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\fbox{$w \equiv 6 \pmod{7}$} \implies \fbox{Friday}
$$

## Casio fx-991CW Calculator "Cheat": 1-Line Zeller's Day Solver

On the **Casio fx-991CW**, you can evaluate the entire Zeller's formula in a single expression using the built-in integer floor function `Int` and `Int÷`.

### Fast Execution in Calculate App

1. Press **HOME** $\to$ select **Calculate** $\to$ press **EXE**.
2. To insert the floor function $\lfloor x \rfloor$:
   - Press **CATALOG** $\to$ **Numeric Calc** $\to$ select **Int** (or **Floor**).
3. Type the expression directly using given parameters $q=25, m=12, K=26, J=20$:
   ```text
   25 + Int(13*(12+1)/5) + 26 + Int(26/4) + Int(20/4) + 5*(20)
   ```
4. Press **EXE** $\to$ Result: `195`.
5. Now press **CATALOG** $\to$ **Numeric Calc** $\to$ **`Int÷`**, and type:
   ```text
   Ans Int÷ 7
   ```
6. Press **EXE** $\to$ Result: `Q = 27, R = 6`.
7. **Read the day code**: $R = 6 \implies$ **Friday**!
