## Vector Norm (Length / Magnitude)

The Euclidean **norm** (length) of a vector $\mathbf{v} = (v_1, v_2, \dots, v_n) \in \mathbb{R}^n$ is given by:

$$
\Huge
\|\mathbf{v}\| = \sqrt{v_1^2 + v_2^2 + \dots + v_n^2} = \sqrt{\mathbf{v} \cdot \mathbf{v}}
$$

### Unit Vector (Normalization)
A vector with norm $1$. Any non-zero vector $\mathbf{v}$ can be normalized:

$$
\Large
\mathbf{u} = \frac{\mathbf{v}}{\|\mathbf{v}\|} \quad \text{where } \|\mathbf{u}\| = 1
$$

---

## Dot Product (Inner Product in $\mathbb{R}^n$)

The **dot product** of vectors $\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v} \in \mathbb{R}^n$ is the scalar:

$$
\Huge
\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = u_1 v_1 + u_2 v_2 + \dots + u_n v_n = \sum_{i=1}^{n} u_i v_i
$$

### Geometric Formula
$$
\Large
\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = \|\mathbf{u}\| \|\mathbf{v}\| \cos(\theta)
$$

Where:
- $\large \theta$ is the angle between $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$ ($0 \le \theta \le \pi$).

---

## Orthogonality

Two vectors $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$ are **orthogonal** (perpendicular, $\mathbf{u} \perp \mathbf{v}$) if and only if their dot product is zero:

$$
\Huge
\mathbf{u} \perp \mathbf{v} \iff \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = 0
$$

---

## Cross Product in $\mathbb{R}^3$

The **cross product** $\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v}$ produces a vector perpendicular to both $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$ in $\mathbb{R}^3$:

$$
\Huge
\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v} = \begin{vmatrix}
\mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\
u_1 & u_2 & u_3 \\
v_1 & v_2 & v_3
\end{vmatrix}
$$

$$
\Large
\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v} = (u_2 v_3 - u_3 v_2)\mathbf{i} - (u_1 v_3 - u_3 v_1)\mathbf{j} + (u_1 v_2 - u_2 v_1)\mathbf{k}
$$

### Geometric Magnitude
$$
\Large
\|\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v}\| = \|\mathbf{u}\| \|\mathbf{v}\| \sin(\theta)
$$

The magnitude $\|\mathbf{u} \times \mathbf{v}\|$ equals the **area of the parallelogram** formed by $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$.

---

## MATLAB Computations

```matlab
% Vectors in R^3
u = [1, 3, -2];
v = [4, -1, 5];

% Vector norm
norm_u = norm(u);

% Dot product
dot_prod = dot(u, v);

% Angle between vectors (radians)
theta = acos(dot(u, v) / (norm(u) * norm(v)));

% Cross product
cross_prod = cross(u, v);

disp(['Dot Product: ', num2str(dot_prod)]);
disp(['Cross Product: ', mat2str(cross_prod)]);
```
