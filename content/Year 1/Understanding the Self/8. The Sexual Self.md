### What is the Difference Between Biological Sex, Gender, and Sexual Orientation?

- **Biological Sex**: Physical assignment at birth based on anatomical/chromosomal features.

- **Gender**: Socially constructed roles, behaviors, attitudes, and identities (includes Gender Expression).

- **Sexual Orientation**: Pattern of emotional, romantic, or sexual attraction (Heterosexuality, Homosexuality, Bisexuality).

### What are the 4 Stages of the Human Sexual Response Cycle?

1. **Excitement**: Initial arousal and physiological preparation.

2. **Plateau**: Physical arousal intensifies.

3. **Orgasm**: Climax/release of sexual tension.

4. **Resolution**: Body returns to normal unaroused state.

### What are the Consequences of Unprotected Sexual Activity?

- **Early Pregnancy**: Miscarriage risks, emotional stress, school disruption, socio-economic burdens.

- **Sexually Transmitted Infections (STIs)**: Syphilis, Gonorrhea, Chlamydia, Genital Warts.

- **HIV/AIDS**: Virus attacking the immune system; transmitted via bodily fluids (blood, semen, breastmilk). Currently uncured but manageable.

### What is Responsible Parenthood and Family Planning?

Family planning allows couples to decide the number and spacing of children (recommended 3–5 years apart).

- **RH Law (RA 10354)**: Guarantees universal access to reproductive healthcare, contraceptives, and sex education in the Philippines.

## Summary

Human sexuality encompasses biological drives, psychological motivations, and cultural constructs. Adolescence brings puberty and physical maturity, making responsible sexual decisions essential. Understanding sexual orientation, practicing safe sex to prevent STIs/HIV, and utilizing birth spacing/family planning methods protect both individual well-being and public health.

## Main Notes

### Sexual Response & Anatomy

- **Primary Sex Characteristics**: Reproductive organs present at birth.

- **Secondary Sex Characteristics**: Features emerging during puberty (voice changes, body hair, breast development).

- **Erogenous Zones**: Areas of heightened sensory sensitivity producing sexual arousal.

### Contraceptive Methods

1. **Natural Methods**:

- Rhythm/Calendar Method, Basal Body Temperature, Cervical Mucus Observation, Lactational Amenorrhea (LAM), Coitus Interruptus (unreliable).

1. **Artificial Methods**:

- Hormonal: Oral pills, patches, injections, implants.

- Barrier: Male/female condoms, diaphragms, cervical caps.

- Devices: Intrauterine Devices (IUD).

1. **Permanent Methods**:

- Vasectomy (males), Tubal Ligation (females).

### 4 Pillars of Family Planning (PH RH Law)

1. **Responsible Parenthood**: Autonomy to decide family size.

2. **Respect for Life**: Protection of life from conception (prohibits abortion).

3. **Birth Spacing**: 3-to-5-year gap between births for maternal recovery.

4. **Informed Choice**: Access to scientifically accurate family planning options.
