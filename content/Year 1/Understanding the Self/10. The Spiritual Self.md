### What is the Spiritual Self?

The innermost, subjective core of selfhood dealing with existential meaning, purpose, and connection to a transcendent reality (_Spiritus_ = breath/life force).

### How Do Religion and Spirituality Differ?

- **Religion**: Organized, shared system of beliefs, rituals, and practices centered on sacred traditions (Durkheim).

- **Spirituality**: Individual, personal search for meaning, purpose, inner peace, and transcendence without requiring adherence to institutional dogmas.

### What are the Key Indigenous Concepts of Soul in the Philippines?

- **Babaylan**: Pre-colonial shaman/priestess acting as healer, medium, and spiritual leader.

- **Dungan**: Conscious, intellectual, and emotional spirit entity.

- **Ginhawa**: Biological breath of life responsible for bodily health and vitality.

## Summary

The spiritual self addresses the human search for existential meaning and transcendent connection. While religions structure these search patterns through public rituals and moral doctrines, personal spirituality operates internally. Indigenous Philippine traditions illustrate this through dual concepts of vitality (_ginhawa_) and spiritual consciousness (_dungan_) guided by spiritual leaders (_babaylan_).
