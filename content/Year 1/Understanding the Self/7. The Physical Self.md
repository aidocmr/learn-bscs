### What Constitutes the Physical Self?

The tangible, concrete dimension of the individual defined by observable physical traits (height, weight, sex, complexion, facial features).

### What Biological Factors Shape Physical Traits?

- **Genotype**: Genetic composition embedded in DNA (not all expressed).

- **Phenotype**: Observable physical features.

- **Chromosomes**: 23 pairs (22 autosomes, 1 sex chromosome pair—XX for female, XY for male).

- **Maturation**: Completion of natural biological growth enabling motor skills and physical changes.

### How Does Culture Affect Body Image and Self-Esteem?

- **Body Image**: Visual perception, feelings, and thoughts regarding one's physical body.

- **Self-Esteem**: Overall evaluation of personal worth.

- Beauty standards are culturally bound (e.g., preference for thinness vs. fullness, skin tones, fitness). Discontent leads to negative self-esteem and risky behaviors (eating disorders like Anorexia/Bulimia).

## Summary

The physical self represents our observable biological structure, determined by genetic inheritance (genotype/phenotype) and natural maturation. However, body image and self-esteem are heavily modified by environmental exposure and media standards. Because beauty standards vary widely across cultures, maintaining a healthy physical self requires balanced nutrition, proper hygiene, and realistic self-acceptance.

## Main Notes

### Biological Foundations

- **Heredity**: Transmission of traits via genes (basic units of heredity).

- **DNA**: Genetic instructions unique to each individual (crucial for parentage verification).

- **Peak Physical Efficiency**: Typically occurs between ages 20 and 30, followed by gradual decline.

### Body Image & Psychological Impact

- **Adolescence**: Puberty triggers rapid physical changes and heightened body consciousness.

- **Cycle of Discontent**: Societal scrutiny $\rightarrow$ Body dissatisfaction $\rightarrow$ Extreme behaviors (steroids, drastic dieting, plastic surgery, eating disorders).

- **Eating Disorders**:
- _Anorexia Nervosa_: Extreme fear of weight gain, severe caloric restriction.

- _Bulimia Nervosa_: Binge eating followed by purging behaviors.

### Cultural Beauty Norms

- Beauty is culturally constructed and subjective.

- Inner beauty (character, health, demeanor) radiates outward to support true physical well-being.
