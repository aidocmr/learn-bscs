### What are the Primary Characteristics of Western Thought?

- Rooted in Greek philosophy, Roman law, and Christianity.

- **Individualistic**: Focuses on autonomy, 'I' self, personal achievements, and competition.

- Values rationalism, logic, personal rights, and linear evolution.

### What are the Primary Characteristics of Eastern Thought?

- **Collectivist**: Focuses on 'We', social harmony, interdependence, and shared roles.

- Seeks self-liberation, dismantling the false self, and achieving unity with nature/cosmos.

### How Do the Four Great Eastern Systems View the Self?

- **Hinduism**: Real nature is divine (_Atman_ = true self; _Brahman_ = ultimate reality). Governed by Karma; goal is realizing _Atman-Brahman_ unity.

- **Buddhism**: _Anatta_ (No-Self/No-Soul). Suffering stems from attachment to illusory selfhood; goal is _Nirvana_ via the Eightfold Path.

- **Confucianism**: Code of ethics and social harmony. Self is relational, developed through moral duty (_Five Cardinal Relationships_).

- **Taoism**: Founded by Lao Tzu. Self is an extension of the _Tao_ (Nature). Selflessness is achieved when distinctions between self and other dissolve.

## Summary

Western and Eastern philosophies offer contrasting frameworks for understanding identity. Western thought emphasizes individualism, rational analysis, personal rights, and self-enhancement. In contrast, Eastern traditions prioritize collectivism, spiritual liberation, social harmony, and unity with the cosmos—viewing the bounded, ego-driven self as an illusion to be transcended or aligned with society and nature.

## Main Notes

### Four Eastern Philosophies

- **Hinduism (Vedanta)**:
- _Atman_: Immutable inner soul.

- _Brahman_: Universal transcendent reality.

- _Karma_: Cause-and-effect principle determining rebirth outcomes.

- **Buddhism (Siddhartha Gautama)**:
- Denies permanent soul (_Anatta_).

- _Four Noble Truths_: Life is suffering $\rightarrow$ Caused by desire $\rightarrow$ Suffering can end $\rightarrow$ Via Eightfold Path.

- _Nirvana_: Extinguishing the false illusion of self.

- **Confucianism**:
- Relational self rooted in social roles.

- _Golden Rule_: Mutual respect.

- _Five Relationships_: Ruler-Minister, Father-Son, Husband-Wife, Brothers, Friends.

- **Taoism (Lao Tzu)**:
- Rejects artificial social roles in favor of natural simplicity.

- _Tao_: Transcendent natural order.

- Ideal state: Absolute selflessness and balance with nature.
