### What is Psychology?

Derived from _Psyche_ (soul/mind) and _Logos_ (study). Scientific study of behavior and mental processes that emerged in the late 19th century.

### Who is the Founder of Experimental Psychology?

**Wilhelm Wundt** established the first experimental psychology laboratory in Leipzig, Germany (1879).

### How Does Psychology Define the Self?

Early psychology (William James) distinguished between:

- **"I"**: The Self as Knower (pure ego/consciousness).

- **"Me"**: The Self as Known (empirical self: physical, social, spiritual).

## Summary

Psychology analyzes the biological, cognitive, and social factors that influence human mental processes and overt behavior. Emerging from philosophy and biology in the late 1800s, it focuses on empirical observation to provide insights into individual behavior, self-awareness, and mental structures.

---

## Main Notes

### Foundations of Psychological Science

- **Scope**: Examines biological influences, social pressures, cognitive structures, and environmental factors.

- **Experimental Milestone**: Wilhelm Wundt (Leipzig, 1879) introduced scientific inquiry into mental processes.

### Key Frameworks

- **Duality of Self (William James)**:
- _"I" Self_: Conscious subject, active thinker, knower.

- _"Me" Self_: Object of perception, empirical self (possessions, roles, qualities).

- **Methods**: Controlled observation, hypothesis testing, neurobiological measurement.
