### What is Self-Discovery vs. Self-Awareness?

- **Self-Discovery**: The process of learning, understanding, or knowing oneself, including one's true potential, motives, and character.

- **Self-Awareness**: Knowledge about oneself and one's worth as a person. Self-awareness is the direct result of engaging in self-discovery.

### How is Personality Defined?

Personality comes from the Greek word _Persona_ (a mask worn by theater actors). It is defined as characteristic patterns of thoughts, feelings, and behaviors that make a person unique.

### What is the Nature vs. Nurture Debate?

- **Nature**: Genetic and biological influences, traits, and heredity (e.g., physical appearance, dispositions).

- **Nurture**: Environmental factors and external influences.

- **Conclusion**: The self is a product of **both** nature and nurture.

### What is the Difference Between Self and Identity?

- **Self**: The person who someone really is.

- **Identity**: Specific characteristics that make a person or group distinct from others.

## Summary

The self is a dynamic, continuously developing construct rather than a static entity. While a name signifies a person, it does not fully encompass who they are. Self-understanding is an ongoing journey influenced by both biological inheritance (nature) and environmental experiences (nurture). While "self" represents our core essential being, "identity" encompasses the unique traits and social markers that distinguish us.

## Main Notes

### Concept & Nature of Self

- **Self**: A continuously molded and developing entity; dynamic and non-static.

- **Self-Discovery**: Process of exploring inner potential, motives, and true character.

- **Self-Awareness**: Realization of personal worth and self-knowledge resulting from self-discovery.

### Personality

- Derived from _Persona_ (Greek for theatrical mask).

- Unique pattern of thoughts, feelings, and behaviors.

### Key Issues of the Self

- **Nature vs. Nurture**:
  - _Nature_: Genetics, heredity, hormones, physical traits.
  - _Nurture_: Environment, upbringing, social exposure.
  - _Synthesis_: Self emerges from the intersection of both.

- **Self vs. Identity**:
  - _Self_: Who a person actually is.
  - _Identity_: Distinguishing characteristics separating an individual/group.
