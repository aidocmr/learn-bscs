### What is Anthropology?

Derived from _Anthropos_ (human) and _Logia_ (study). It is the holistic science of human origin, physical traits, cultural developments, and contemporary variations across time and space.

### What are the 4 Main Branches of Anthropology?

1. **Archaeology**: Reconstructs past human life through physical remains/artifacts.

2. **Physical/Biological Anthropology**: Focuses on human biological evolution, genetics, and adaptation.

3. **Linguistic Anthropology**: Studies language origins, structures, and cultural impact on communication.

4. **Cultural Anthropology**: Examines cultural diversity, customs, and social practices of present/past societies.

## Summary

Anthropology examines what makes human beings unique by analyzing biological and cultural developments across history. Through its four distinct branches, anthropology establishes that human identity is deeply intertwined with linguistic patterns, historical context, evolutionary biology, and cultural practices.

## Main Notes

### Fundamentals of Anthropology

- Definition: Science of human origins, diversity, and cultural development.

- Primary focus: Explaining biological and cultural similarities/differences across human groups.

### Four Subfields

- **Archaeology**: Analyzing material culture and prehistory artifacts.

- **Physical/Biological**: Biological diversity, human genetics, evolution.

- **Linguistic**: Evolution of speech, language structure, cultural context of communication.

- **Cultural**: Ethnographic study of living societies and historical social systems.
