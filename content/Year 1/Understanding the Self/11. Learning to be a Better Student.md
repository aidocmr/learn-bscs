### What is Learning and How Does It Occur?

Learning is a purposeful, active, multifaceted process resulting in enduring behavioral and cognitive changes through experience, practice, or education.

### What is Outcome-Based Education (OBE)?

Pioneered by James Spady, OBE prioritizes what students can _do_ and perform practically over passive knowledge retention.

### What is Self-Regulated Learning?

The ability of a student to independently plan, monitor, control, and evaluate their own learning process without requiring external intervention.

## Summary

Effective learning requires shifting from passive memorization to active, meaningful engagement. Self-regulated learners establish organized habits, reflect on their understanding, and apply new knowledge to practical real-world contexts. Frameworks like Outcome-Based Education emphasize measurable competencies and practical problem-solving.

## Main Notes

### Characteristics of Effective Learning

- **Purposeful**: Directed toward practical skill development.

- **Multifaceted**: Involves memory, motor skills, logic, and emotional regulation.

- **Active Process**: Requires direct practice and personal engagement.

### Study Strategies for Success

- **Get Organized**: Schedule dedicated review times and goal markers.

- **Study to Understand**: Focus on conceptual understanding rather than rote repetition.

- **Feynman Technique / Peer Teaching**: Explain concepts to others to uncover knowledge gaps.

- **Self-Testing**: Use flashcards and practice assessments to verify recall.
