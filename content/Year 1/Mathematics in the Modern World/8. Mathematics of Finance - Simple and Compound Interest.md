## Interest Rate

### Nominal Interest Rate

> Expressed as: $\large r$

Stated or advertised interest rate. Typically expressed on an annual basis. **Ignores the effect of compounding.** 

### Effective Interest Rate

> Expressed as: $\large i$

The **true annual rate** that is paid or earned. It takes into account the compounding of interest during the year.

### Compounding Frequency

> Expressed as: $\large m$

 - Annually: $m = 1$
 - Semi-annually: $m = 2$
 - Quarterly: $m = 4$
 - Monthly: $m = 12$
 - Daily: $m = 365$

## 1. Simple Interest

Simple interest is calculated solely on the initial principal amount over a given time:

$$
\Huge
I = Prt
$$

### Future Value (Maturity Value)

$$
\Huge
F = P + I = P(1 + rt)
$$

Where:
- $\large I$ is the **Simple Interest** earned or paid
- $\large P$ is the **Principal / Present Value**
- $\large r$ is the **Annual / Nominal Interest Rate** (expressed as a decimal)
- $\large t$ is the **Time in years**
- $\large F$ is the **Future Value / Maturity Value**

$$
\textit{eg. Borrowing } \$5,000 \text{ at } 6\% \text{ simple interest for } 3 \text{ years:}\\
\large
\begin{align*}
I &= 5000 \cdot 0.06 \cdot 3 = \$900\\
F &= 5000 + 900 = \$5,900
\end{align*}
$$

## 2. Compound Interest

Interest computed on the principal and on the accumulated interest from previous compounding periods:

$$
\Huge
F = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{m}\right)^{mt} = P(1 + i)^n
$$

Where:
- $\large m$ is the [[#Compounding Frequency]]:
- $\large i = \frac{r}{m}$ is the [[#Effective Interest Rate]]
- $\large n = mt$ is the **Total number of compounding periods**

### Present Value Under Compound Interest

$$
\Large
P = \frac{F}{(1 + i)^n} = F(1 + i)^{-n}
$$

### Worked Example

$$
\begin{align*}
F&=\ ?\\
P&= 12,000\\
j&= 0.15\\
t&= 4\\
\end{align*}
$$

$$
i = \frac{i}{m} = \frac{0.15}{4} = 0.0375
$$

$$
n = tm = 4(4) = 16
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
F&=P(1 + i)^{n}\\
F&=(12,000)(1 + 0.0375)^{16}
\end{align*}\\

\fbox{ $F \approx 21,626.73$ }
$$

---

## 3. Continuous Compounding

When compounding frequency $m \to \infty$:

$$
\Huge
F = Pe^{rt}
$$

Where in $e \approx 2.71828\dots$ is Euler's constant.

---

## 4. Effective Annual Rate (EAR)

The actual annual rate of return when compounding occurs more than once per year:

$$
\Huge
\text{EAR} = \left(1 + \frac{r}{m}\right)^m - 1
$$
